08
The engineering under the UI

Serious engineering, where it matters for money.

Six guarantees modeled on real-world financial constraints — not a demo feature list.
Deterministic authority
Six rules — fraud, terminal, duplicate, customer limit, cooldown, spend cap. Fixed order, fail-closed, and the optimizer can never widen the allowed set.
policy_gate :: LLM_output → ALLOW/DENY
Append-only audit trail
Every recommendation, decision, and execution is recorded — decisions are written before they execute, and the full loop is reconstructable.
decisions · claims · outcomes → immutable
Idempotency
A durable claim keyed to (event, intervention) decides which single attempt crosses the money-moving boundary. No double-charges, no double-spend.
PK(event_id, intervention) → one side-effect
Bounded spend
Intervention costs and a daily spend cap bound what RecoveryOS can spend chasing a recovery — plus a 30-minute cooldown and 2-per-customer limit.
daily_spend_cap = ₹50,000 · cooldown 30m
Fail-closed verification
Signatures verified over the raw body before any parsing. An invalid or missing webhook signature can never change state.
HMAC-SHA256 · constant-time · reject 4xx
Stateful recovery lifecycle
Recovery is a tracked state machine — not a fire-and-forget call. Outcomes move waiting → pending → verified only on evidence.
PENDING_OUTCOME → RECOVERED
